# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS with minimal vanilla JavaScript, as requested. GitHub Pages is the requested deployment target.

## Users

Restaurant, catering, hotel, clinic, nursing-home, and butcher-shop buyers sourcing products at wholesale prices.

## Product Purpose

Let wholesale buyers browse the supplied price list, choose products and quantities, review their order, and send it directly to La Granja Don Silvio on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Orders are assembled from the supplied wholesale catalog and handed to the sales team as a ready-to-review WhatsApp message.

## Operating Context

Buyers compare products and prices on desktop or mobile, add quantities in kilograms or units where the catalog specifies them, and send an order request for confirmation by WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show every product in the supplied wholesale catalog, grouped by category.
- Use the supplied prices as indicative, VAT-inclusive prices that can change without notice.
- Free delivery starts at ARS 400,000, subject to delivery zones and days; payment is in advance.
- Send the selected products, quantities, and applicable subtotal to WhatsApp at +54 9 11 2801-1111.
- The catalog gives prices per kilogram except chicken by the crate, whose price must be confirmed.
- No checkout, payment processing, live stock, or verified delivery-zone data was supplied.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the La Granja Don Silvio name, supplied logo, and established identity from the linked brand site.
- Follow the brand reference's deep-green, cream, and meat-red palette and its direct, approachable voice.

## Product Principles

- Preserve every catalog item and its supplied price or “consultar” status.
- Make product selection and quantity changes clear and quick.
- Present the cart as an order request, not as a completed purchase.
- Confirm pricing, availability, and delivery details with the sales team.
